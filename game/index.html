EMBER COURT

A card duel of wits — outplay Duchess Vex across three lanes.

Each turn you gain embers and draw a card. Play cards into the Ember, Tide or Gale lane. When the round ends, the stronger side of each lane wins it and deals the difference as damage. Two cards of one element in a lane resonate: each gains +1 power. Reduce the rival court to 0 influence first.

Keyboard: 1–7 pick a card · 1–3 pick a lane · Enter ends the turn
